How to use the fx-991MS calculator

A simple guide. No maths knowledge needed. Open the calculator

1. Start here

  • Tap the keys, or use your keyboard on a computer (numbers, + − * /, Enter, Backspace, Esc).
  • On your phone: open the page in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). After one visit it also works without internet.
  • To get a new version, open the app once with internet. If the keys look old, close the app completely and open it again.

2. Reading the screen

The top line shows what you typed. The bottom line shows the answer. The tiny letters at the very top tell you what is switched on:

SSHIFT is on (the next key uses its yellow label)
AALPHA is on (the next key uses its red letter)
hypThe next sin, cos or tan will be the hyperbolic one
STO / RCLWaiting for you to press a letter key (see section 6)
MSomething is saved in memory M
D / R / GAngle unit: Degrees, Radians or Gradians. Press MODE to change it. Use D for everyday angles.

3. Your first sums

  1. Press 7 + 8 =. You see 15.
  2. Press AC, then 2 + 3 × 4 =. You see 14, because × is done before +, like in school.
  3. Use ( and ) to change the order.
DELRemoves the last thing you typed
ACClears everything
◀ ▶Move the cursor to fix a mistake in the middle
AnsUses your last answer. After =, pressing + or × continues from the answer automatically
(−)A negative number, like −5

4. Yellow and red labels

Many keys do two or three jobs. The yellow word above a key is used after SHIFT. The red letter is used after ALPHA. Press SHIFT or ALPHA first, then the key.

Example: 5 0 SHIFT = = gives 0.5. SHIFT + = is the percent sign (50%).

6. Memory (saving numbers)

  • Save a number: get it on the screen (for example 8 =), press SHIFT RCL (that is STO), then the key with the red letter you want, such as (−) for A.
  • Use it later: ALPHA (−) × 2 = gives 16. Or press RCL then the letter key.
  • Red letters: A on (−), B on hyp, C sin, D cos, E tan, F RCL, X on ), Y on (, M on M+.
  • M+ adds the number to memory M. SHIFT M+ takes it away. SHIFT AC (CLR) erases all memory.

7. The extra row: calculus, angles, degrees-minutes-seconds

To type the letter X, press ALPHA then ).

  • ∫dx (area under a curve): ∫dx ALPHA ) x² , 0 , 3 ) = gives 9. That is the area of X² from 0 to 3.
  • d/dx (steepness at a point): d/dx ALPHA ) SHIFT x² , 2 ) = gives 12 (X³ at X = 2).
  • Pol( turns x,y into distance and angle: Pol( 3 , 4 ) = shows 5 ∠ 53.13010235. Rec( does the reverse.
  • Degrees, minutes, seconds: SHIFT on ∫dx, d/dx, Pol( gives ° ′ ″. Type 2 SHIFT ∫dx 1 5 SHIFT d/dx 3 6 SHIFT Pol( = to get 2.26. Press ▶DMS after an answer to show it as 2°15′36″.

8. The panels

These buttons open a small window. Fill in the boxes, tap Calculate, read the answer, tap Close.

EQNSolves equations. Pick the type, fill in the numbers. For x² − 3x + 2 use a = 1, b = −3, c = 2 and you get x1 = 2, x2 = 1. Answers with i are complex numbers.
STAT / REGStatistics. Type one number per line to get the average (mean), σ and s. Type pairs like 1,2 per line to get a best-fit line (A, B, r). Try the numbers 1 to 5: mean 3, σ 1.414213562.
BASE-NNumber systems. Type 255, choose DEC, and you see HEX FF, BIN 11111111, OCT 377. You can also use AND, OR, XOR, NOT and more.
CALCRuns a formula again and again. Type A*X+2, set A = 3 and X = 4, and you get 14. You may type * and / and words like sqrt( and pi.
SOLVEFinds X in an equation. Type X^2=9 with a start guess of 1 and you get X = 3. Try a different guess to find the other answer (−3).
CONSTFamous constants like the speed of light. Pick one and tap Insert into expression.
CONVUnit conversion, such as in → cm, mile → km, °C → °F. Type a value and tap Calculate.
CMPLXComplex numbers (a + bi). (1 + 2i) × (3 + 4i) gives −5 + 10i.
MATMatrices. One row per line, numbers separated by spaces. Matrix 1 2 / 3 4 has determinant −2.
VCTVectors. A = 1,2,3 and B = 4,5,6: the dot product is 32.

9. If something looks wrong

  • Math ERROR means the sum cannot be done (for example dividing by zero, or a missing number). Press AC, or press ◀ to fix what you typed.
  • Answers show up to 10 digits.
  • This is a recreation, not the real Casio. The panels use boxes instead of the real menus, CONST and CONV have fewer entries, and BASE-N works in 32 bits.
